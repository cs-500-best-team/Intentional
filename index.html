Download for Android · 11.8 MB Android 8.0 or later · Prototype for testing · Not an iPhone app

From download to your first check-in

  1. Open the downloaded APK. If Android asks, allow this browser to install unknown apps, then confirm installation.
  2. Open Intentional and read the automatic study collection notice. After continuing, choose Try the 75-second demo to explore without special access.
  3. For Instagram and YouTube check-ins, choose Set up opening check-ins and enable Intentional in Accessibility settings.

If Android blocks accessibility for a downloaded app, open Intentional’s Android App info, then its menu and Allow restricted settings, if available. Return to Accessibility settings afterward. Device wording varies.

Automatic study collection

After the one-time notice, your study records are sent automatically to the research team’s private Firebase service when configured. Records include pseudonymous identifiers, app openings/timestamps, intentions and extension reasons, selected/actual times, relaxation ratings, and fulfillment answers. No manual sending is needed. Intentional reads only the focused app’s package name, not posts or messages. Your phone’s speech provider may process voice input online.

Already installed? Install over the existing app to keep your journal. If Android reports a conflicting signature, ask the sender for a build signed with the original key; uninstalling removes local data.

Done trying the prototype?

Tap Uninstall Intentional at the bottom of any app screen, then confirm removal with Android. You can also press and hold the app icon and choose App info → Uninstall.

Removing Intentional stops its check-ins and future uploads and deletes its local journal. Unsynced changes may be lost; data already received by the study team is not erased. Instagram and YouTube stay installed.
